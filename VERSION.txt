SYNERGOX SITE — BUILD 6  (lead-magnet funnel + free book)
=========================================================

NEW IN THIS BUILD

  The free book — public/growth-playbook.pdf
    "The Compounding Business" — a 74-page premium guide.
    Seven growth levers (Offer -> Traffic -> Conversion -> Ascension ->
    Retention -> Referral -> Compounding), 8 chapters, a 9-tool toolkit
    (checklists, audits, weekly scorecard, 90-day plan), and a close
    that transitions to your done-for-you offer.

  The opt-in funnel
    Hero primary CTA is now "Get the free growth playbook".
    It opens a modal (components/OptIn.tsx):
        first name + email
        -> POST lead to FORM_ENDPOINT
        -> success screen, book opens in a new tab
        -> 3-second countdown
        -> redirect to PAYMENT_URL
    Any component can open it via useOptIn() (OptInProvider.tsx).

  Analytics — lib/analytics.ts
    Fires 5 funnel events (lead_email_submitted, lead_book_sent,
    redirect_to_payment, payment_page_viewed, purchase_completed)
    to dataLayer/gtag, with a safe no-op fallback.

BEFORE IT EARNS MONEY — set two things in lib/site.ts
    PAYMENT_URL     paste your real checkout link (Paystack/Flutterwave/
                    Stripe). Falls back to /apply until you do.
    FORM_ENDPOINT   your Formspree/Getform/Basin URL.

EMAILING THE BOOK AUTOMATICALLY
    A static Vercel site can't email a file by itself. The opt-in opens
    the book instantly AND posts the lead; the auto-email is one
    connection in an email tool. Full step-by-step (MailerLite/Brevo
    free tier) is in README.md -> "Emailing the book automatically".

Page order (unchanged):
  hero, problem, method, teardown, paths, services, journey, proof,
  difference, trust, questions, lead capture, guarantee, final CTA
