SYNERGOX SITE — BUILD 4  (qualification gate)
=============================================

New in this build:
  /apply  — ten-question qualification flow, currency first,
            then the Calendly embed with answers prefilled.

Every "book a call" button now routes to /apply instead of
straight to Calendly: hero, nav, teardown, both path cards,
final CTA, mobile sticky bar, footer, services and learn pages.

Question order:
   1. Currency          (asked BEFORE any money question)
   2. Market
   3. Business model
   4. Monthly revenue   (bands shown in chosen currency)
   5. Monthly ad spend  (bands shown in chosen currency)
   6. Biggest bottleneck
   7. What they've tried
   8. Timeline
   9. Decision authority
  10. Contact details

Lowest revenue band + no ad spend routes to /learn instead of
the calendar, with a "book anyway" link still offered.
