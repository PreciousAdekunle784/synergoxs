SYNERGOX SITE — BUILD 9  (no-tab redirect + conversion payment page)
====================================================================

WHAT CHANGED FROM BUILD 8

  1. NO MORE DISTRACTING NEW TAB
     Submitting the opt-in no longer force-opens the book in a new tab
     (that pulled people out of the funnel). Now:
        submit -> success screen (~3s, with a countdown)
               -> AUTO-REDIRECT to the payment page (/offer)
     The book still gets delivered — by email — so nothing is lost.
     A "Take me there now" button lets impatient people skip the wait.

  2. PRICE = N199,999
     Set as the default in lib/site.ts
     (NEXT_PUBLIC_OFFER_AMOUNT_KOBO = 19999900 kobo).
     Change it in that one place / via env.

  3. NEW CONVERSION PAYMENT PAGE — /offer  (app/offer/page.tsx)
     A long-form, direct-response sales page for your PAID
     done-for-you offer, written in a Hormozi / Suby / Brunson style:
        pattern-interrupt hero
        the real problem (knowing != doing)
        what we build (6 concrete deliverables)
        value stack + price anchoring (N199,999 vs N2.45M+ of value)
        risk reversal (ship-it, own-it, no lock-in)
        real proof (Kings Food Mart only — no invented quotes)
        objection handling (the 3 real hesitations)
        final close
     The Paystack INLINE checkout button (components/PayButton.tsx)
     appears three times down the page and prefills the email
     captured at opt-in. Falls back to /apply if Paystack isn't set.

FLOW NOW
  any CTA -> opt-in modal -> subscribe fn (save lead + email book)
          -> success (3s countdown) -> /offer (sales page)
          -> Paystack button -> purchase_completed -> /guide

THE BOOK IS FREE. /offer sells the paid done-for-you build. The book
is delivered by email the moment someone opts in.
